## 🛠️ How to Buy & Connect with Hostinger

Once you select your domain, here is the setup workflow:

### Step 1: Purchase on Namecheap
1. Go to [Namecheap.com](https://www.namecheap.com/).
2. Search for your chosen name (e.g. `buildstationai.com` or `buildstation.ai`).
3. Add to cart. Ensure **Domain Privacy** (WhoisGuard) is toggled **ON** (free for life on Namecheap).
4. Apply Namecheap promo codes (e.g., `NEWCOM698` for new accounts on `.com`).

### Step 2: Connect Namecheap to Hostinger
You have two easy ways to point your Namecheap domain to Hostinger:

#### Method A: Change Nameservers (Recommended by Hostinger)
In your Hostinger hPanel under **Websites → Manage**, look for Hostinger's nameservers:
- `ns1.dns-parking.com`
- `ns2.dns-parking.com`

In Namecheap:
1. Go to **Domain List** → Click **Manage** next to your domain.
2. Under **Nameservers**, change from *Namecheap BasicDNS* to **Custom DNS**.
3. Enter Hostinger’s two nameservers and click the green checkmark.
4. DNS propagation typically completes within 15–60 minutes.

#### Method B: Keep Namecheap DNS & Point via A Record
In Namecheap:
1. Go to **Advanced DNS**.
2. Add an **A Record**:
   - Host: `@` | Value: *[Your Hostinger Website IP Address]* | TTL: Automatic
3. Add a **CNAME Record**:
   - Host: `www` | Value: *[yourdomain.com]* | TTL: Automatic
